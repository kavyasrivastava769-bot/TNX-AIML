# Week 1 — Monday 12 Oct 2026 to Monday 19 Oct 2026

## Week 1 goal

Week 1 puts every member on their chosen track with a **working setup**, a **first hands-on
milestone**, and **one filled-in worksheet per day**. Week 1 is the first week and runs **Monday to
Monday**: Day 1 to Day 7 are the topic days (Mon to Sun) and the final Monday is a review day.
**From Week 2 onward, weeks run Wednesday to Monday.**

By the final Monday:

- **Track A** can write and run small Python programs using variables, types, strings, input/output,
  operators, and `if` / `elif` / `else`.
- **Track B** knows exception handling, file handling, OOP, and the advanced-Python concepts
  (comprehensions, lambdas, iterators, generators), and has built the Bank Management System project.
- **Track C** has brushed up **numpy, pandas, and matplotlib** (one library per day) and covered the
  **ML fundamentals**: what ML is, the types of ML, EDA, data cleaning, preprocessing, and feature
  engineering/selection, plus the two foundation projects.

## 7-day schedule (Mon → Sun) + review Monday

Each day has the same shape per track: a block of the source video (with timestamps), a hands-on
**deliverable**, and the matching **day worksheet**. The final Monday has no new topics.

| Day | Date | Track A — Python Basics | Track B — Advanced Python | Track C — ML Fundamentals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Mon 12 Oct | Setup + first program | Exceptions + file handling | numpy |
| Day 2 | Tue 13 Oct | Comments, variables, data types | File-handling project + OOP intro | pandas |
| Day 3 | Wed 14 Oct | Strings & type conversion | OOP: inheritance & polymorphism | matplotlib & seaborn |
| Day 4 | Thu 15 Oct | Input & output | Advanced Python: comprehensions & lambdas | What ML is, types of ML |
| Day 5 | Fri 16 Oct | Operators | Advanced Python: iterators & generators | EDA & data cleaning |

| Review | **Mon 19 Oct** | **Review, catch-up, worksheet only** | **Review, catch-up, worksheet only** | **Review, catch-up, worksheet only** |

### Source material this week

- **Tracks A and B** both use one course:
  [Python Full Course for Beginners to Advanced (12 hours)](https://youtu.be/_aWbUudZ5Yo).
  Track A watches from the start to the end of the **If-Else** chapter (0:00 → 2:16:14). Track B
  watches from **Exception handling** to the end (6:23:24 → 11:34:50). The exact timestamped links are
  in each track's `checklist.md`.
- **Track C** brushes up the libraries with one video per day — numpy, pandas, then matplotlib &
  seaborn — and then takes the **ML fundamentals** from the
  [Complete Machine Learning Course playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLaldQ9PzZd9qT0KsKJ7yCq70iFFP3MFJ5),
  starting with **Part 1 — Foundation**. The library videos each come with a dataset and a capstone
  worksheet in their description (linked in the checklist).

## How to choose a track

- **Track A** is for you if you know zero or minimal coding, or just want to brush up the basics.
- **Track B** is for you if you are comfortable with variables, functions, and loops, and want to level
  up toward Python libraries.
- **Track C** is for you if you have some Python and want to start machine learning, beginning with a
  numpy / pandas / matplotlib brush-up.

Ask your AIML lead if you are unsure — we can help you pick, or switch once at any Monday.

## Submitting your work

The full fork → checklist → worksheet → WhatsApp walkthrough is in
[`SUBMISSION.md`](../SUBMISSION.md). Short version — your work is **not** edited in the upstream repo.
In your own fork:

1. Open your track's `checklist.md` and tick off each `- [ ]` as you finish it.
2. Open that day's `worksheet/day-N.md`, fill in your answers, and save (commit) your changes.
3. Share the link to your filled-in worksheet in your team channel.
